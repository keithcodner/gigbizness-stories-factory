THE GREAT BRICK HEIST

Runtime: Approximately 3 minutes
Style: Fast-paced comedy, mystery, and family-friendly action

CHARACTERS
BENJI BLOCKS - A determined YouTuber who investigates strange stories.
MR. GREEDWELL - The dramatic CEO of MegaBrick Mart.
MAYA - A clever 12-year-old Lego builder.
DAD - Maya's protective but slightly confused father.
OFFICER STUDS - A serious police officer who loves terrible brick-related jokes.

SCENE 1 - THE MISSING COLLECTION

INT. FAMILY BASEMENT - DAY

Shelves once filled with colourful Lego sets are completely empty. Maya stares at the bare walls in shock.

MAYA:
They're gone! The castle, the pirate ship, even Grandma's limited-edition space station!

DAD:
Who steals Lego from a family?

Maya notices a gold business card on the floor.

MAYA:
"MegaBrick Mart. We build profits."

DAD:
Mr. Greedwell was here yesterday. He offered to buy the collection, but I told him it wasn't for sale.

Maya grabs her phone.

MAYA:
We need someone who investigates powerful people.

She pulls up a video titled: "I Spent 24 Hours Inside a Fake Toy Store."

MAYA:
We need Benji Blocks.

SCENE 2 - THE YOUTUBER INVESTIGATES

EXT. FAMILY HOUSE - AFTERNOON

Benji jumps out of a van covered in cameras, lights, and giant red arrows.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Benji Blocks here. Today, we're investigating the strangest brick mystery I've ever seen.

DAD:
Can you help us get the collection back?

BENJI BLOCKS:
I once found a missing sandwich using only a receipt and three security cameras. I was born for this.

Maya hands him the gold business card.

MAYA:
Greedwell dropped this.

Benji examines it.

BENJI BLOCKS:
There's red dust on the corner.

MAYA:
The MegaBrick warehouse has red floors.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Then that's where we're going.

He turns toward the camera.

BENJI BLOCKS:
For legal reasons, we are investigating, not breaking in.

A beat.

BENJI BLOCKS:
We're sneaking in very respectfully.

SCENE 3 - INSIDE THE EVIL WAREHOUSE

INT. MEGABRICK WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

Benji and Maya hide inside oversized delivery boxes. Nearby, Mr. Greedwell admires the stolen Lego collection.

MR. GREEDWELL:
At last! Once I sell these rare sets, MegaBrick Mart will control the entire collector market!

ASSISTANT:
Sir, you don't have a moustache.

Greedwell quickly clips on a fake one.

MR. GREEDWELL:
Every evil CEO needs proper branding.

Inside the box, Benji secretly records him.

MAYA:
He admitted everything.

BENJI BLOCKS:
And we have it on video.

Benji's phone suddenly plays a loud notification.

PHONE:
Congratulations! Your video has reached one million views!

Everyone freezes.

MR. GREEDWELL:
Who's there?

Benji and Maya burst out of the boxes.

BENJI BLOCKS:
It's over, Greedwell! Give the family back their Lego.

MR. GREEDWELL:
Never! Security!

Two toy-store mascots enter, trip over loose bricks, and fall harmlessly into a pile of cushions.

SCENE 4 - THE BRICK CHASE

INT. WAREHOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Greedwell loads the rare sets onto a rolling cart and races toward the exit.

MR. GREEDWELL:
You'll never catch me! This cart has premium wheels!

Benji grabs a Lego-built scooter.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Good thing Maya believes in custom builds.

Maya presses a button. The scooter launches forward.

MAYA:
It also has turbo mode!

Benji catches up and swings his camera onto Greedwell's cart.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Smile for the thumbnail!

Greedwell panics and crashes into a giant display shaped like a Lego police station.

Real police officers enter through the doors.

OFFICER STUDS:
Mr. Greedwell, you're under arrest for theft.

MR. GREEDWELL:
You can't prove anything!

Benji holds up the camera.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Actually, you gave us a full confession, a villain speech, and three different camera angles.

OFFICER STUDS:
Looks like your plans just fell to pieces.

Everyone groans.

SCENE 5 - THE COLLECTION RETURNS

INT. FAMILY BASEMENT - DAY

The Lego collection is back on the shelves. Maya places the final minifigure onto the castle.

DAD:
You really got everything back.

MAYA:
Even Grandma's space station.

Officer Studs leads Greedwell past the basement window in handcuffs.

MR. GREEDWELL:
I would have gotten away with it if it weren't for that YouTuber and his tiny plastic scooter!

Benji faces his camera.

BENJI BLOCKS:
A powerful CEO thought he could take something that didn't belong to him.

Maya steps beside him.

MAYA:
But having money doesn't put you above the law.

BENJI BLOCKS:
And stealing Lego is a terrible investment strategy.

Maya hands Benji a small custom minifigure made to look like him.

MAYA:
For helping us.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Does it come with camera equipment?

MAYA:
No. It comes with a lawyer.

Benji looks directly at the camera.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Smart kid.

TEXT ON SCREEN:
THE COLLECTION WAS SAVED.

BENJI BLOCKS:
Like, subscribe, and always check your security cameras.
