THE GREAT BRICK HEIST

Runtime: Approximately 3 minutes
Style: Fast-paced comedy, mystery, and family-friendly action

CHARACTERS
BENJI BLOCKS — A determined YouTuber who investigates strange stories.
MR. GREEDWELL — The dramatic CEO of MegaBrick Mart.
MAYA — A clever 12-year-old Lego builder.
DAD — Maya's protective but slightly confused father.

SCENE 1 - THE MISSING COLLECTION

INT. FAMILY BASEMENT - DAY

Shelves once filled with colourful Lego sets are completely empty. Maya stares at the bare walls in shock.

MAYA:
They're gone! The castle, the pirate ship, even Grandma's limited-edition space station!

DAD:
Who steals Lego from a family?

Maya notices a gold business card on the floor.

MAYA:
"MegaBrick Mart. We build profits."

DAD:
Mr. Greedwell was here yesterday. He offered to buy the collection, but I told him it wasn't for sale.

Maya grabs her phone.

MAYA:
We need someone who investigates powerful people.

She pulls up a video titled: "I Spent 24 Hours Inside a Fake Toy Store."

MAYA:
We need Benji Blocks.
